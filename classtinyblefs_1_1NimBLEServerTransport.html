Connection interval the server asks the client for, in units of 1.25 ms (default 6..6 = 7.5 ms, the shortest).

The central decides, and a Bluetooth 4.0 PC controller picks the upper value of the range: with 6..12 it gave 4.7 KB/s, with 6..6 10.5 KB/s. A long interval saves power. Call before begin().
